01 · Logo System

Concept

An interlocked "O" (ring) and "D" (spine + bowl) built from pure structural geometry. A roofline chevron above and a foundation line below turn the monogram into a miniature building elevation — strong, geometric and unmistakably construction. The mark is fully vector (SVG) so it scales cleanly from a favicon to a hoarding.
